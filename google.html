Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal voice and text assistant that one person built for their own use. It runs on its owner's computer. It is not a product for sale and is not offered to the public.

What it does with Google

When its owner connects it, Jarvis can:

  • read and summarise Gmail messages
  • write email drafts
  • check Google Calendar

Sending an email or booking an event always needs the owner's spoken yes and a click on a confirm button.
